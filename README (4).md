## What is Adya?

Adya AI (brand of Shayr Omnichannel Pvt. Ltd.) is an agentic AI company. Its offering is **fifteen studios arranged in five layers, forming one agentic fabric**. The three flagship platforms at the top are what you will sell most often.

### The three flagship platforms

| Platform | What it does | Typical buyer |
|---|---|---|
| **SAI** – Agentic SDLC | The Super AI orchestrator at the heart of the Vanij platform; builds the full software development lifecycle from a prompt | Engineering and product leaders |
| **RAI** – Agentic GTM | Runs go-to-market: discovers and scores leads, runs email and LinkedIn campaigns, creates content, runs ads and tracks performance | Marketing, sales and growth leaders |
| **RAS** – Agentic Research, Analysis & Strategy | Turns a business question into researched, analysed and strategic answers, cutting the days spent gathering and reading documents | Research, strategy, investment and life-sciences teams |

The ICP and buyer-persona files for each platform are in [Know Adya](#1--know-adya), Section B.

### The 15 studios, by layer

| Layer | Studios |
|---|---|
| **L5 · Platforms** | SAI (full SDLC) · RAI (go-to-market) · RAS (research and strategy) · Karma (a personal agent) |
| **L4 · Channels** | WhatsApp Studio (agents on WhatsApp) · Voice Studio (agents on the phone) · Communications (Slack, Teams, Discord) |
| **L3 · Deploy and share** | Cloud Studio (any cloud, including yours) · Marketplace (publish, sell and learn) |
| **L2 · Builders** | AIA (agents and workflows on demand) · Coding Agent (38 sub-agents that ship code) |
| **L1 · Foundations** | Model Studio (models on demand) · AI ELT (any data, agent-ready) · ESM (memory that learns) · AGP (governance as code) |

### Headline proof points

- Ranked on global benchmarks: **#3 BIRD-Bench** (natural language to data, RAS), **#1 agentic memory** (ESM), **#6 SWE-Bench** and **#8 Terminal-Bench 2.0** at launch in March 2026 (Coding Agent); currently **#12 on the Terminal-Bench 2.0 global leaderboard**
- **7 patents filed**, six more being filed
- **~96,000 developers**, about 15,000 actively building
- Deployed across **India, the US and Africa**

*Source: "Adya – Fifteen Studios, One Agentic Fabric" deck. Check the latest version before quoting figures to a customer.*
