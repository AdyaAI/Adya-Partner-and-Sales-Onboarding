## What is Adya?

Adya AI (brand of Shayr Omnichannel Pvt. Ltd.) is an agentic AI company. Its offering has two layers:

| Layer | What it is | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Platforms** | Ready-to-deploy AI solutions you sell to customers | **SAI**, **RAI** (AI GTM agent), **RAS** (Research, Analyze & Strategize), **Cloud** |
| **Studios** | 15 building blocks/toolkits that power Adya solutions | Model Studio, AGP, WhatsApp Studio, Voice, and 11 more |

You don't need to master everything on day one. The onboarding path below introduces the big picture first, then each product, then the commercial material.
